What is Concrete?

Concrete is a DeFi protocol that automatically earns you yield on your crypto. Think of it like a smart savings account — you deposit your tokens, and Concrete moves them around different lending platforms to find you the best interest rate at all times. It was built by Blueprint Finance and is backed by Polychain Capital, YZi Labs (formerly Binance Labs), and VanEck. At its peak it held over $1 billion in deposits.

How yield works

When you deposit into Concrete, your money doesn't just sit there. Concrete spreads it across lending platforms like Aave, Compound, Morpho, and Pendle — wherever the interest rate is highest. A system called the Allocator watches rates constantly and moves your funds automatically the moment a better rate appears. Concrete also uses quantitative models — similar to hedge fund math — to predict market conditions and optimize where your money goes. They claim this beats doing it manually by 12 to 18 percent per year.

ctTokens explained

When you deposit into Concrete you receive a special token called a ctToken. If you deposit USDT you get ctUSDT. If you deposit ETH you get ctETH. This token is your receipt — it proves you own a share of the vault. As the vault earns yield, every ctToken automatically becomes worth more of the original asset. You don't receive separate interest payments — the value of your ctTokens quietly grows over time. When you want your money back, you redeem your ctTokens and receive your original deposit plus all the yield earned.

Example:

Deposit 1,000 USDT → receive 1,000 ctUSDT → after one year at 8.5% → redeem for ~1,085 USDT

4

Vault types

Concrete has different vaults depending on what you deposit and your risk preference. The main product is the USDT DeFi Vault launched with Binance Wallet, offering around 8.5% APR with a 100 USDT minimum and a 7-day withdrawal window. Vanilla lending vaults are the simplest option — they just lend your tokens on Aave or Compound and earn 2 to 3 percent per year with lower risk. Institutional vaults for larger depositors charge a slightly reduced fee of 1.25 percent per year. The yield rate is not fixed — it changes with market conditions.
5

Fees

Concrete charges no deposit fee, no withdrawal fee, and no performance fee. The only cost is a 1.5 percent annual management fee on your deposited amount. On $10,000 that is $150 per year. This fee is not taken from your wallet directly — instead new vault shares are quietly minted to the fee recipient, which dilutes all holders slightly over time. Institutional vaults pay a reduced fee of 1.25 percent per year.

Risks

Like all DeFi, Concrete carries risks. Smart contract risk: the protocol is code and could have bugs — it has been audited by Trail of Bits, Halborn, and Code4rena, but audits don't guarantee safety. Variable yield: the 8.5% rate is not a promise — it changes daily. Withdrawal delay: async vaults have a 7-day wait before you get your funds back. Strategy risk: Concrete uses many underlying platforms — if one of them fails, it could affect your funds. This is not a bank — there is no deposit insurance.

Token & Bags airdrop

Concrete has not launched a token yet. To reward early users, they run a points system called Bags. You earn Bags by connecting your wallet, completing social tasks on X, depositing into vaults, using the protocol, and referring friends — all at points.concrete.xyz. When Concrete eventually launches their token, Bags are expected to convert into an airdrop allocation. A $200,000 token rewards campaign ran for 21 days and ended April 2, 2026 — early depositors earned the largest shares. Exact tokenomics have not been officially disclosed.

Who is behind it

Concrete is built by Blueprint Finance, a team focused on institutional-grade DeFi infrastructure. Their backers include Polychain Capital (one of the largest crypto venture funds), YZi Labs (previously Binance Labs — the investment arm of Binance), and VanEck (a major traditional finance asset manager). They raised $7.5 million in a seed round and $9.5 million in a strategic round, totalling over $17 million. Partners include Binance Wallet, Figment, Arbitrum, Hypernative (security monitoring), and TRES Finance (accounting verification). Their sister product on Solana is called Glow Finance.
